🏷️ ABC analysis & stock policy

Items are ranked by yearly spend. The few A items that make up 80% of spend get the most protection against running out; cheap C items get less, so money isn't locked in low-value stock. Safety stock = z × how much weekly use varies × √lead time, where z comes from the service level.

🧪 Policy simulation: old rule vs ClinicIQ

Replays 8 weeks of usage for all items, starting from today's stock. Old rule: reorder at the fixed shelf-card level, order 2 × that level. ClinicIQ: reorder at forecast demand over the lead time + ABC safety stock, order up to 4 more weeks.

Prototype note: sample data. Stock-outs in the first week come from today's low stock, before any order can arrive.

🎯 Forecast accuracy check

How it works: for every item, the last 4 weeks are hidden, each method forecasts them from the previous 8 weeks, and the forecast is compared with what was actually used. Error = total forecast miss ÷ total actual usage (WAPE), so lower is better. ClinicIQ then uses the most accurate method for each item.

Prototype note: usage history is sample data, so these results show the method, not real clinic accuracy. The "best method per item" figure is slightly optimistic, because the method is chosen on the same 4 weeks it is scored on; with real data you would confirm it on the following weeks.
